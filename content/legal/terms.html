5. Mudras

5.1 What mudras are

Mudras are Paartha’s virtual reward currency. They are a feature of the Services, not money, a security, a deposit or e-money.

5.2 How you earn them

You earn mudras by studying. At the time of writing:

  • Daily check-in streak: your first study activity of the day extends your streak and pays mudras that grow with the streak (25, 50, 100, 200 and then in steps up to a daily maximum of 300).
  • Notes and videos: 1 mudra for each note or video the first time you complete it.
  • Important questions: 5 mudras once per unit for working through all its important questions.
  • Quizzes: 10 mudras for scoring above 90% on your first attempt at a unit quiz; if you did not, 5 mudras for scoring above 90% on your second attempt. Later attempts do not earn mudras.

Each reward can be earned once. We may change the amounts and the ways to earn at any time; changes do not remove mudras you already hold.

5.3 Promotional and effort-based mudras

Mudras are of two kinds. Effort-based mudras are the ones you earn by studying. Promotional mudras are ones we give you, for example as a welcome gift or in a campaign. Promotional mudras may pay for at most 250 mudras (₹25) of the price of each course. Mudras you earned by effort, and mudras you obtained in other ways we describe in the App or on the Website, can pay for up to the whole price of a course, as the checkout shows.

5.4 Using mudras

Mudras are redeemed at checkout against the price of a course. 10 mudras are worth ₹1 off. The checkout shows exactly how many mudras are used and what is left to pay. Mudras you have used on an order that is later refunded are returned to your balance (or, if we choose, refunded in rupees where that is how they were obtained).

5.5 Getting mudras another way

Where we offer it, mudras may also be obtained through our Website. We do not sell mudras inside the iOS App. Mudras obtained any way are subject to this section.

5.6 What mudras are not

  • Mudras have no cash value and cannot be sold, transferred, gifted, traded or withdrawn.
  • They stay on your account only while your account exists and is in good standing.
  • We may remove mudras obtained through misuse, fraud, bugs we correct, or breach of these Terms. Using scripts, multiple accounts or other tricks to earn mudras or streaks is prohibited.
  • If we ever introduce an expiry for mudras, we will tell you in the App well before it applies.
