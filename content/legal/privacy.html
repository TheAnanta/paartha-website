Paartha Privacy Policy

Effective: October 24, 2026

Paartha is committed to being usable by everyone. If you need this policy in another format, a different language or in plain words, write to paartha@theananta.in and we will help, at no cost.

At Paartha, we think a study app should earn your trust before it asks for anything. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information The Ananta (“Paartha”, “we”, “us”) collects when you use our mobile apps for Android and iOS (the “App”) and our website at paartha.theananta.in (the “Website”), together with our courses, rewards, support and other features (the “Services”), why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

“Personal information” means any information about an identified or identifiable person. For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”), The Ananta is the “Data Fiduciary” for the personal information described here and you are the “Data Principal”.

The short version. We collect what we need to run your account, unlock the courses you buy, show you how you are studying and talk to you when you write to us. We do not sell your personal information, we do not show third-party advertising, and we do not use advertising trackers in the App. You can see and delete parts of your data, or your whole account, in the App under Profile → My Data.

Depending on how you use Paartha, some parts of the Services may have their own short notices (for example, a camera or photo prompt on your phone, or a notice shown when you first turn on a feature). Those notices add to this policy. Links in the Services may lead to websites or apps run by others; this policy does not cover them, and we encourage you to read their policies. Signing in with Google or Apple is also covered by their privacy policies.

1. Collection of personal information

We may collect or process the following personal information, from what you give us, from what we receive from the sign-in providers and others, and from what the App collects automatically as you use it.

a. Information you provide to us

  • Account and identity. When you sign up we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google or Apple (Apple may let you hide your email). We also ask for your university roll number, which we use to work out your semester and enrol you in the courses meant for you.
  • Profile. A profile picture you choose to upload (from your photo library or camera), your language preference and the settings you choose (for example notification settings).
  • Study content you create. Bookmarks and favourites, flashcards you mark to revisit, your answers to quizzes, and anything you write in a review.
  • Reviews and ratings. The rating and comment you write for a course.
  • Support conversations. Messages you send to our support team, and images you attach to them, together with the ticket details.
  • Suggestions. Course suggestions and feedback you send us.
  • Coupons and cart. Courses you add to your cart and coupon codes you enter.
  • Payment information. When you pay, the course, amount, GST, status, payment ID and the method used (for example UPI, card or net banking). For UPI payments we keep the UPI ID that appears on the receipt. Your card number, UPI PIN and bank login are handled only by our payment partner Razorpay; we never see or store them.

b. Information from others

  • Google and Apple sign-in. The identifier, name, email address and profile picture they pass to us after you sign in.
  • Our payment partner. Confirmation of whether a payment succeeded, its reference and method.
  • Your university or batch (where applicable). If your university or an instructor arranges access for a batch, we may receive the roll numbers or course assignments needed to give you that access.

c. Information we collect automatically

  • Device identifier. A random ID that the App creates on your phone. We use it to keep an account to a limited number of devices and to protect it from misuse. It is not your advertising ID and is not linked to other apps.
  • Study activity. Which videos, notes, important questions, flashcards, mind maps and quizzes you open, for how long, what you completed, your quiz scores, and your resulting progress and streaks.
  • Rewards. Your mudras balance and the history of what earned or used them.
  • Notifications. Your push-notification token (from Google/Apple), your notification choices, and the notifications we have sent you.
  • Technical data. App version, device type and operating system, platform (Android or iOS), approximate time zone, and the IP address and time of requests to our servers, used for security, fixing faults and counting how the Services are used.
  • Wellbeing activities. That you opened or finished a short wellbeing activity. We do not record what you type inside them as health data; anything you enter in an exercise stays on your phone unless an exercise says otherwise.

d. What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, your microphone recordings, your precise location, or the contents of your photo library (we receive only a picture you actively choose to upload). The App has no third-party advertising or behavioural-tracking tools.

e. De-identified and aggregated data

We may combine information into statistics that no longer identify you (for example, how many students finished a unit) and use them to improve courses. This is not personal information and is not covered by this policy.

2. Use of personal information

We use your personal information to:

  • create and secure your account, sign you in, and keep an account to its permitted devices;
  • show you the right courses for your semester, unlock the courses you buy or are given, and deliver videos, notes, questions, quizzes and other content;
  • show your progress, streaks and the For You page, make your study plan, and recommend what to study next;
  • run our rewards programme (deciding which mudras you earn and applying them at checkout);
  • process payments, check them with our payment partner, issue receipts and sort out failed or disputed payments and refunds;
  • send notifications you have not switched off, and the service notifications we must send (replies from Support and go-live alerts for pre-registered courses);
  • answer your questions, handle complaints and give you support;
  • show your reviews to other students, and show your profile picture where described in section 3;
  • maintain, fix and improve Paartha, understand which content helps students most, and develop new features;
  • keep Paartha safe: detect and prevent fraud, abuse, account sharing, cheating on rewards, and security incidents;
  • comply with law, respond to lawful requests, and keep records we must keep (such as tax and accounting records); and
  • establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Lawful grounds

Under the DPDP Act we process your personal information (i) with your consent, which you give when you sign up, when you choose to upload a picture or turn on notifications, and in other places where we ask (you can withdraw it at any time; see section 6), and (ii) for legitimate uses the Act allows without separate consent, such as providing a service you asked for or paid for, meeting a legal obligation, responding to a medical emergency or a threat to life, and keeping Paartha safe from fraud. For people outside India, we rely on consent, on performing our contract with you, on our legitimate interests (running, securing and improving Paartha, in ways that do not override your rights) and on legal obligations.

Automated processing

Some features are automatic: for example, deciding the next resource in your study plan, or whether a quiz score earns mudras. These follow fixed rules about your own activity. We do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

5. Data security and retention

Security

We take your information’s security seriously and follow generally accepted practices. Connections between the App and our servers are encrypted in transit; every request to our servers carries a sign-in token that is checked; access to our databases and storage is limited to the people and services that need it and protected by credentials; payment card and UPI details never reach us. In line with the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 and the DPDP Act we maintain reasonable safeguards against unauthorised access, loss, alteration and disclosure. No system is perfectly secure. If something happens that affects your personal information we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires. If you suspect that your account has been misused, contact us at once at paartha@theananta.in.

6. Your privacy rights

We believe you should control your personal information. Wherever you live, you can ask us to do the following, using the tools in the App or by writing to paartha@theananta.in:

  • Access. Know what personal information we hold about you, how we use it and who we share it with. Profile → My Data shows each category and how much of it we hold, and we can send you a summary or copy on request.
  • Correction and updating. Ask us to correct inaccurate information or complete incomplete information (for example your name or roll number).
  • Erasure. Delete parts of your data under My Data (study activity, reviews, support conversations, notifications, cart), or delete your whole account there. Deleting your account removes your profile, mudras and study history. Some records we must keep, such as payment records, stay as explained in section 5.
  • Withdraw consent. You can withdraw a consent you gave at any time (for example by turning off notifications, removing your profile picture, or deleting your account). Withdrawal does not affect what we did before, and some features may stop working without the information they need.
  • Portability. Ask for your personal information in a commonly used, machine-readable format, where that is practical.
  • Restriction and objection. Ask us to limit or stop certain uses, such as optional offers; we will tell you if we cannot because the use is needed to run your account or the law requires it.
  • Grievance redressal. Complain to us about how your information is handled (section 11) and expect a reply within the times the law sets.
  • Nominate someone. Name a person who can exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.
  • No retaliation. We will not treat you worse for using these rights.

To protect you, we may ask for information to confirm that a request is really from you (we will normally just ask you to write from your signed-in account’s email). We aim to respond within 30 days, and sooner for simple requests; if we need more time we will tell you why. We do not charge for requests unless they are clearly excessive or repetitive. Your rights are not absolute: exceptions may apply for our legal obligations, the rights of others, security, and our ability to bring or defend legal claims. If we decline a request we will explain why.

7. Children’s privacy

Paartha is made for university students and is intended for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly offer Paartha to anyone under 13 and do not knowingly collect their personal information. A person under 18 may use Paartha only with the verifiable permission of a parent or legal guardian, who agrees to these terms on their behalf. For any account we know belongs to a person under 18, we do not track or monitor behaviour for advertising and we do not show targeted advertising (we do not show it to anyone). If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us personal information without your permission, write to paartha@theananta.in and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Cookies and the Website

  • The App. The App does not use cookies. It stores a few things on your phone: your sign-in session, your device identifier, your settings, and recently loaded content so that screens open quickly.
  • The Website. Our Website is an information site. At the time of writing it uses only the technical cookies and storage that are strictly necessary for pages to load and does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If we add analytics or any non-essential cookies, we will update this section and ask for your consent first where the law requires. Our web server records standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.
  • Your browser lets you block or delete cookies; blocking essential ones may stop parts of a site from working.

9. Where your information is processed

Our application servers run on Google Cloud in India (Mumbai region). Some of our providers, such as Google (Firebase), Apple, Cloudflare and our database host, may process or store information in other countries, in which case that information is subject to the security standards of those providers and to the laws of those places. The DPDP Act permits transfer of personal data outside India except to countries the Government of India restricts; we do not send your information to any restricted country. Where we use a provider outside India we choose reputable providers and require them to protect your information.
